About Me

Hello, I am Diego Hernandez. From a young age, I have always wanted to make a big impact, whether that was in my community or through the world.

I am 14 years old, I go to Alonso High School, and I take part in the International Baccalaureate (IB) program.

I care about my community, so join me to advocate for what is right and what benefits our community.

My Mission

I believe leadership means showing up, listening, and doing the work. My goal is to use my voice and actions to improve lives in Tampa and beyond.

Leadership & Experience

School and Achievement

  • Represented my school in the Hillsborough County Student Forum with the Board of Education.
  • Student government participation.
  • Multiple academic awards, including an academic excellence award from the President for education.

Community Action

  • Active in the community, not only talking about issues but helping solve them.
  • Cleaned and picked up waste from parks, streets, and trails around Tampa.
  • Advocated for equal rights, justice for immigrants, women's bodily autonomy, affordable healthcare, transparency in government, and climate change awareness.

Issues I Care About

  • The current injustice of the Department of Homeland Security.
  • Immigrant rights.
  • Climate change.
  • Using taxpayer money responsibly and not for wars.
  • Enforcing global alliances.
  • Shutting down data centers.
